Questions

Before you ask.

Can I play Brawlverse right now?

Not yet. This page is the first thing we've shipped. The playable build is in development, and the GitHub repository is where it will appear first.

What kind of game is it?

A platform brawler. Hits raise your opponent's damage, higher damage means longer launches, and you take a stock by knocking them off the stage. Last fighter with stocks left wins.

What will I need to play?

A modern desktop browser with WebGL. There is nothing to install. The plan is keyboard and gamepad support; phones are not a launch target.

How will playing with a friend work?

You open a room, send the link, and your friend joins from their browser. The match runs peer to peer with rollback netcode, so it stays responsive without a game server in the middle. Two players on one keyboard is planned too.

Is this based on an existing game?

No. The genre is familiar, but every fighter, stage, name and piece of art here is original to Brawlverse.

How were the visuals on this page made?

The key art and the opening and closing footage were generated with Google Flow. The fighter and stage scenes are rendered live in your browser with Three.js, using depth maps to give the art real geometry.

Who is building it, and can I follow along?

It's an independent project built in the open. Star or watch the repository on GitHub to see progress as it lands.

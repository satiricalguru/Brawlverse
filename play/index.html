How to play
  • Attack plus a direction picks the move: tilts on the ground, aerials in the air. Smash attacks charge while held.
  • Shield plus left or right rolls, plus down dodges in place. In the air, shield is an air dodge.
  • Down on a platform drops through it. Down while falling fast-falls.
  • Hanging on a ledge: jump, up to climb, down to let go.
  • M mutes the sound, ` shows hitboxes and the frame readout.
